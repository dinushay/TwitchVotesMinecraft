## 2024-05-24 - Dual-Channel Feedback in Minecraft
**Learning:** Minecraft UI often relies solely on visual indicators (BossBars, chat messages) which can easily be missed during intense gameplay or by players with visual impairments.
**Action:** Pair important state changes (like phase changes and countdowns) with auditory cues (`org.bukkit.Sound` events) to provide robust dual-channel feedback.
